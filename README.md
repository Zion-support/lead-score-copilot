## What it does

Lead Score Copilot combines website behavior, email engagement, firmographics, and intent data into one explainable score — so reps call the leads most likely to close, first.

## Key features

- **Real-time scoring** — scores update the moment a lead acts.
- **Explainable factors** — reps see exactly why a lead is hot.
- **Fit + intent split** — ICP fit and buying intent scored separately.
- **CRM-native routing** — hot leads auto-assigned with Slack alerts.
- **Model feedback loop** — scores retrain on your closed-won data.

## Who it's for

Sales and marketing teams with more inbound volume than rep hours.
